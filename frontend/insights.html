ResNet18 Vision Backbone

Processes 224x224 chest radiographs through deep convolutional residual blocks. Frozen early layers maintain robust low-level spatial features while layer3/4 are fine-tuned for thoracic opacities. Extracts a dense 512-dimensional latent embedding.

Radiology Explainability (Grad-CAM) Spatial Localization

Grad-CAM uses the gradients of the target concept flowing into the final convolutional layer (layer4[-1]) to generate a coarse 2D localization map highlighting the regions in the chest radiograph most critical for predicting consolidation.

Clinical Signal Attribution (SHAP) Game Theoretic

SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) computes Shapley values from cooperative game theory to quantify the exact marginal contribution of each symptom and vital sign toward the tree ensemble's final disease log-odds.
